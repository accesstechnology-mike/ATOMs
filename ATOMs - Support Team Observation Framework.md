# **ATOMs \- Support Team Observation Framework** 

Our clients do best, and AT fails less often when our clients’ support networks are well-equipped to help them.   

We know that our clients’ successes with AT are reliant on a network of interdependencies, human to human, human to tech, tech to human, tech to tech.  When these interdependencies are present, reliable and work in support of our client’s intentions… anything is possible.  We call this ‘**The Golden Bubble of Joy**’.

access: technology defines ‘The Golden Bubble of Joy’ as:

*“A network of **reliable interdependencies** that has the ability to **confidently support and enhance the quality of life.”***

access: technology recognises the place that we as AT professionals have within our client’s ‘Golden Bubble of Joy.’  With this in mind, the purpose of this framework is to consider and plan how our clients’ AT outcomes can be enhanced through the increased confidence and ability of the support network. 

The actions you consider may be for yourself or a colleague. You may recognise the need for additional company-wide resources, training and/or service development as a result of your observations. This is welcomed and encouraged. Actions may be celebratory or involve sharing of best practice.

# Quality of Life considerations

## *The client’s support network are:*

## Understanding of client’s needs

| 1 | Care is taken to ensure the client is physically and emotionally ready to learn and/or engage in activities.  The support team recognises the social, environmental and procedural barriers that hinder client success and work collaboratively to overcome them. Support team prioritise and are extremely responsive to clients physical, social and emotional needs |
| :---: | :---- |
| **2** | Clients physical, social and emotional wellbeing are well considered and planned for.   Client perspectives and opinions are considered and drive supporting activities where it is safe and appropriate to do so.  |
| **3** | Support is driven by policy, outcome or process. Further action is required to ensure client’s social, emotional and/or physical wellbeing is considered and supported. |
| **Notes** |   |

## 

## *The client’s support network are:*

## Supportive of client intentions

| 1 | Client is encouraged and/or able to regularly advocate for themselves, receives advocacy support or has an advocate that acts on their behalf Support is committed to hearing, understanding and validating client’s wishes, opinions and intentions. Plans for support and intervention are based on the client's wishes and intentions, with clients directing their own support/goals where possible. |
| :---: | :---- |
| **2** | Opportunities are created to hear, understand and validate clients opinions, wishes and areas of interest. Support team is responsive to client’s communication and behaviour. |
| **3** | Opportunities are missed to hear and/or understand client’s own opinions, wishes or areas of interest.  Little support and/or value given to the exploration of client’s areas of interest  |
| **Notes** |   |

## 

## *The client’s support network:*

## Facilitate client autonomy and independence

| 1 | Client is happy and makes excellent progress as a result of highly skilled, respectful and responsive support. Client has excellent relationship with support team and is empowered to direct and make decisions about their own support.  Client’s health, safety and wellbeing are well considered. Client is enabled to make contributions and be part of decision making processes where possible. |
| :---: | :---- |
| **2** | Client has a good relationship with their support team and is enabled to make age/capacity appropriate choices and contributions. Support is skilled in identifying when a client can do things for themselves and when they need additional support. Client is generally happy and makes good progress toward goals Client’s health and safety is well considered. |
| **3** | Opportunities to enable client choice making are missed or are not age/capacity appropriate. Client’s progress, wellbeing and/or safety is hindered by over or under-support. |
| **Notes** |   |

## *The client’s support network have:*

## Respectful & fair age/capacity-appropriate expectations

| 1 | Expectations are aspirational and are respectful of client's age, interest, cognitive ability and physical ability.   Communication is positive and is supportive of client's rights to explore and express their identity. |
| :---: | :---- |
| **2** | Expectations are reasonable, and consider the client's age, interest, cognitive and physical abilities. Communication is appropriate and respectful. |
| **3** | Expectations are too high/too low and do not take into consideration the client’s rights and freedoms relative to their age, cognitive and/or physical abilities.  Communication is inappropriate, patronising, lacking positivity and/or aspiration.  |
| **Notes** |   |

#  AT buy-in

## *The client’s support network:*

## Recognise the positive impact AT can have

| 1 | Demonstrates an excellent awareness of the potential ways that AT can improve the quality of life of all disabled people and has high aspirations for what the client can make possible. |
| :---: | :---- |
| **2** | Recognises the potential ways that AT can improve the client’s quality of life and is committed to supporting the client to achieve their goals. |
| **3** | Requires further opportunity to learn about the benefits of AT and support to build confidence in its ability to enhance the client’s life. |
| **Notes** |   |
| **Action Required** |  |

## 

## *The client’s support network:*

## Understand and demonstrate AT best practice

| 1 | Has the confidence and insight to develop solutions that support client intentions beyond current intervention and recommendations, without the support of an AT professional.  Is comfortable and equipped to motivate the client during periods of non-linear progress. |
| :---: | :---- |
| **2** | Can deliver effective support using AT, developing client skills and knowledge drawing upon support from AT professionals as required.  Is able to motivate clients to achieve outcomes, is not process or tech focussed. |
| **3** | Relies on support put forward by AT professional to deliver intervention to client. Finds it hard to action recommendations  Is unsure of AT journey e.g. might expect AT to have an immediate positive impact, endpoint etc. |
